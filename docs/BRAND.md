## Trademark — what to do, in order `[D · general knowledge of NZ/CN process; verify at iponz.govt.nz and cnipa.gov.cn]`
1. **Search first.** IPONZ trade mark search (New Zealand) for "dot dot dot", "dotdotdot", "…" in classes **9** (software), **41** (video production/education), **42** (SaaS). Do the same at CNIPA (中国商标网) and WIPO Global Brand Database. Punctuation-only marks ("…") are weak; the word mark + the three-dot logo together are stronger.
2. **File in NZ** as a word mark *and* a device mark (logo) in classes 9, 41, 42. IPONZ offers a pre-application search/advice report; use it. Budget order of magnitude: a few hundred NZD per class.
3. **China**: file the English word mark + the logo in classes 9/41/42 via a Chinese agent; consider a Chinese-character mark (连点成线) too, because CN examiners treat it separately. China is first-to-file — do this early if 抖音/小红书 are core channels.
4. Keep the logo files, first-use dates (this repo's git history) and screenshots of first publication as evidence.
5. Don't announce the name publicly before the domains are bought and the NZ application is lodged.
